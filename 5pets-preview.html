What You're Seeing

All 5 pets share the same archetype (builder), same progress (1.0 / complete), and same personality parameters. The only difference is the pet ID, which produces a different SHA-256 seed.

The PRNG seed drives: variant selection (beard detail positions), '?' pixel fill (50% chance), accent color shifts (ANSI ±6), and motif overlays (10% body pixel accent swap).

Notice: the overall silhouette is identical across all 5. Variations are limited to minor color shifts and small pixel detail differences. This is the diversity problem that LLM generation addresses.
